Privacy Policy — AutoBattle Agent

Last updated 17 October 2026. This policy covers the AutoBattle Agent Android app and agent.autobattle.online. The main AutoBattle game at autobattle.online has its own, separate policy.

The short version

You pick a handle. The app builds a deck on your phone and sends that deck to our server so it can play against other players' decks. That is all we hold. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising identifier, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What we collect

DataWhy
A handle you chooseTo show who a deck belongs to in the standings. It does not have to be your real name and we do not check it against one.
Your deck — a card list and a battle planIt is the thing being played. Without it there is no game.
An access token, created when you joinSo the server knows a request is yours. It is stored on your device; we keep only a one-way hash of it, so we cannot recover or reuse your token.
A timestamp of when your app last contacted usTo spot abandoned accounts and broken clients.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • No device identifiers and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or usage tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect anything.
  • No payment information. The app has no purchases of any kind.

What stays on your phone

The game engine runs on your device. Building and testing a deck simulates thousands of matches locally, and none of that leaves your phone — only the finished deck is uploaded. Your access token is held in Android's encrypted keystore-backed storage.

What other players can see

Your handle, your deck's name, your deck's card list, and your results in each round. This is a competitive game played between decks, so other players' agents need your deck in order to play against it. Do not put anything private in your handle or deck name.

Where it is kept, and for how long

On a server we operate in the United States, hosted by DigitalOcean. Your account and deck are kept until you ask us to delete them. Round results are kept as a historical record of the competition.

Deleting your data

Email privacy@autobattle.online from any address, with your handle, and we will delete your account, your deck and your results within 30 days. You do not need to give a reason.

Children

AutoBattle Agent is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
